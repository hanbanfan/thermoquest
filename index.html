🧠 Thermodynamics Brain Map

WORLD 1 — What am I looking at?
System → surroundings → boundary → closed system → control volume
WORLD 2 — How do I describe it?
Properties → intensive → extensive → specific → state → equilibrium
WORLD 3 — What is it doing?
Process → cycle → steady → transient → quasi-equilibrium
WORLD 4 — Temperature & Pressure
Kelvin → Rankine → absolute pressure → gauge pressure → manometers
WORLD 5 — Where is the energy?
Internal energy → kinetic energy → potential energy → heat → work
WORLD 6 — Energy moving through devices
Mass flow → control volumes → efficiency
WORLD 7 — Water gets weird
Pure substance → saturation → mixture → latent heat → critical point
